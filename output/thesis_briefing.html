1. Executive Summary & Core Research Objectives

This investigation presents a high-fidelity astrodynamics simulation suite coupled with an edge-deployable Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) / Deep Learning Surrogate for Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellites. Ground truth is generated via high-order adaptive numerical integration (Runge-Kutta Dormand-Prince RK45) incorporating geopotential zonal harmonics (J2) and dynamic thermospheric aerodynamic drag. The ML surrogate model is trained on non-dimensionalized transition state pairs to enable real-time orbit propagation on resource-constrained satellite flight computers (e.g. ESP32 microcontrollers).

2. Meeting Talking Points for Prof. Ferrer

  • Mathematical Rigour & Integrator Baseline: Ground truth trajectories are generated strictly using high-order variable step-size numerical methods (DOP853/RK45) with local error tolerances of 10−9.
  • Dual Perturbation Coupling: Simultaneously models conservative gravitational J2 field effects (nodal precession and apsidal rotation) and non-conservative atmospheric drag (co-rotating atmosphere with altitude-dependent scale heights).
  • Surrogate Paradigm Shift (Residual Transition Map): Rather than learning absolute whole-trajectory positions (which fail to generalize across multi-orbit rollouts), the neural network learns a one-step residual velocity/position flow field: St+Δt = St + Nθ(St). This shrinks network size to <10k parameters (≈ 36 KB fp32, ≈ 9 KB int8), maintaining <5% rollout drift over 24 hours.
  • Physics-Informed Loss Enforcement: Employs PyTorch auto-differentiation (torch.autograd) to penalize violations of Newton's second law directly in backpropagation.
  • Automated Parametric Sweep Engine: Supports automated Monte Carlo and parametric sweeps across ballistic coefficients (B = m / (CdA)), orbital inclination, eccentricity, and perigee altitudes.

3. Thorough Theoretical Formulation & Governing Equations

3.1. Fundamental Equations of Motion (Two-Body with Perturbations)

In the Earth-Centered Inertial (ECI J2000) frame, the state vector S(t) = [r(t)T, v(t)T]T ∈ ℝ6 evolves according to:

dr/dt = v

dv/dt = − (μ⊕ / ||r||3) r + aJ2(r) + adrag(r, v) + a3B(r, t)

where μ⊕ = 3.986004418 × 1014 m3/s2 is Earth's geocentric standard gravitational parameter.

3.2. Geopotential Oblateness (J2) Zonal Harmonic

Earth's equatorial oblateness generates a non-spherical gravitational acceleration field derived from the gradient of the zonal gravitational potential UJ2:

aJ2(r) = − (3/2) · (J2 μ⊕ R⊕2 / r4) · [ (x/r)(1 − 5 z2/r2),   (y/r)(1 − 5 z2/r2),   (z/r)(3 − 5 z2/r2) ]T

Analytical Secular Rates for Model Validation: The secular drift rate of the Right Ascension of the Ascending Node (dΩ/dt) and Argument of Perigee (dω/dt) serve as analytical validation truth:

dΩ/dt = − (3/2) n̄ J2 (R⊕ / p)2 cos i

dω/dt = + (3/4) n̄ J2 (R⊕ / p)2 (5 cos2 i − 1)

where n̄ = √(μ⊕ / a3) is mean motion, p = a(1 − e2) is the semi-latus rectum, R⊕ = 6378.137 km, and J2 = 1.08263 × 10−3.

3.3. Aerodynamic Drag in a Co-Rotating Thermosphere

The drag acceleration opposes the satellite's velocity vector relative to the rotating atmosphere (vrel):

vrel = v − ω⊕ × r

adrag = − (1/2) · ρ(h) · (CdA / m) · ||vrel|| vrel = − (1/2) · (ρ(h) / B) · ||vrel|| vrel

where ω⊕ = [0, 0, 7.292115 × 10−5]T rad/s, B = m / (CdA) is the Ballistic Coefficient (kg/m2), and ρ(h) = ρ0 exp(−(h − h0) / H) with segmented scale heights H(h).

3.4. Non-Dimensionalization & Normalization Scaling

To prevent gradient vanishing and numerical divergence during deep learning backpropagation, canonical units map all variables to Ο(1):

r̃ = r / Rscale, &quad; ṽ = v / vscale, &quad; t̃ = t / Tscale
(with Rscale = 6,378,137 m,   vscale = 8,000 m/s,   Tscale = 5,400 s)

3.5. PINN Loss & Physics Residual Optimization

The composite loss function penalizes empirical data discrepancy alongside differential equation residuals:

Ltotal = Ldata + λphys · Lphysics

Ldata = (1/N) ∑ || S̃k+1 − Ŝk+1 ||2

Lphysics = (1/N) ∑ [ || ∂r̂/∂t − v̂ ||2 + || ∂v̂/∂t − atotal(r̂, v̂) ||2 ]
