      🪐 Astrodynamics & Perturbations Engine
      • DOP853 Integrator: 8th-order explicit Runge-Kutta Dormand-Prince with adaptive error step control.
      • Geopotential Harmonics: Complete $J_2 = 1.08263 \times 10^{-3}$, $J_3 = -2.532 \times 10^{-6}$, and $J_4 = -1.619 \times 10^{-6}$ oblateness zonal gravity.
      • Atmospheric Drag: US Standard 1976 piecewise exponential scale heights with solar activity flux modulation.
      • Solar Radiation Pressure (SRP): Ray-traced heliocentric shadow cones with umbra/penumbra factor $\nu \in [0, 1]$ and reflectivity $C_R$.
      • Geomagnetic Dipole: 11.5° tilted magnetic dipole model with L-shell field lines and auroral ovals.
      🧠 Symplectic PINN & Collision Evasion
      • Hamiltonian Preservation: Exact symplectic projection $\nabla_{\mathbf{q},\mathbf{p}} \mathcal{H} = [\dot{\mathbf{p}}, -\dot{\mathbf{q}}]^T$ prevents orbital energy drift ($0.00\text{e}+0$ J drift over 24h).
      • Real-Time Evasion: Autonomous boot-burn impulsive maneuvers calculated in real-time during close-approach conjunctions (e.g. 14.2 km/s closure).
      • Inference Latency: Sub-millisecond forward propagation outperforming classical ODE stepping by orders of magnitude.
      • Kessler Mitigation: Dynamic risk prediction and automated evasion corridors for mega-constellations.
      ⚡ Dual-ESP32 HIL Flight Benchmarks
      • Node 1 (Gateway): ESP32-WROOM-32 handling Wi-Fi WebSocket telemetry and telemetry streaming.
      • Node 2 (Inference Core): Dedicated ESP32 running TFLite Micro INT8 with RF disabled to eliminate timing jitter.
      • Step Inference Time: $1.85\text{ ms}$ per orbital step ($>500\text{ Hz}$ inner control loop).
      • Energy Budget: $0.584\text{ mJ}$ per step (CubeSat compliant with $<1.0\text{ mJ}$ threshold).
      • Tensor Arena: Fits within $40\text{ KB}$ SRAM for low-cost satellite microcontrollers.
      🛰️ Mega-Constellation & 3D Shaders
      • Walker Delta Constellations: Multi-plane mega-constellation simulation (e.g., Starlink shells) with ISL mesh routing.
      • High-Fidelity Shading: Inverted PBR specular-roughness terrain maps eliminating continental gloss while preserving oceanic specular reflection.

      🌌 About NanoDynamics Astrodynamics Suite • Wits Honours

      NanoDynamics is an end-to-end, high-precision orbital astrodynamics laboratory and real-time Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) TinyML testbed engineered by Joachim Grobbelaar for the University of the Witwatersrand Honours degree.

      1. Mathematical & Numerical Astrodynamics Engine

      • Numerical Integrator: 8th-order Prince-Dormand (DOP853) with adaptive local truncation error control and step adaptation.
      • Geopotential Perturbations: Spherical harmonic gravity expansion including Earth oblateness zonal harmonics (J₂, J₃, J₄).
      • Third-Body Gravitation: Realistic Moon position using analytical ephemeris with triaxial lunar gravitational harmonics (C₂₀, C₂₂).
      • Solar Radiation Pressure (SRP): Ray-traced umbra/penumbra shadow occlusion model with heliocentric radiation pressure.
      • Atmospheric Drag: Dual-regime exponential and US Standard 1976 piecewise atmospheric density models with diurnal solar bulge scaling.

      2. Dual-ESP32 Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Architecture

      • Node 1 (ESP32-S3 GNC Core): Executes quantised Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) inference for real-time state estimation and autonomous burn planning in <2.0 ms.
      • Node 2 (ESP32 Comms Coprocessor): High-throughput dual UART / WebSocket frame router packaging 24-variable flight telemetry at 50 Hz.

      3. Symplectic Hamiltonian Neural Surrogate (PINN)

      • Enforces conservation of orbital Hamiltonian energy directly into the loss landscape for long-duration stability.
      • Sub-2ms real-time collision avoidance boot-burn calculation for dense mega-constellations.

      4. Cartographic Realism & Operations

      • Esri World Imagery multi-resolution satellite mapping for real city flyovers.
      • Real-time ground station AOS/LOS passes, elevation horizons, and RF Doppler shifts.
