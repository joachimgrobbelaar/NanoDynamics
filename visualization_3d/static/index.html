      🌌 About NanoDynamics Astrodynamics Suite • Wits Honours

      NanoDynamics is an end-to-end, high-precision orbital astrodynamics laboratory and real-time Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) TinyML testbed engineered by Joachim Grobbelaar for the University of the Witwatersrand Honours degree.

      1. Mathematical & Numerical Astrodynamics Engine

      • Numerical Integrator: 8th-order Prince-Dormand (DOP853) with adaptive local truncation error control and step adaptation.
      • Geopotential Perturbations: Spherical harmonic gravity expansion including Earth oblateness zonal harmonics (J₂, J₃, J₄).
      • Third-Body Gravitation: Realistic Moon position using analytical ephemeris with triaxial lunar gravitational harmonics (C₂₀, C₂₂).
      • Solar Radiation Pressure (SRP): Ray-traced umbra/penumbra shadow occlusion model with heliocentric radiation pressure.
      • Atmospheric Drag: Dual-regime exponential and US Standard 1976 piecewise atmospheric density models with diurnal solar bulge scaling.

      2. Dual-ESP32 Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) Architecture

      • Node 1 (ESP32-S3 GNC Core): Executes quantised Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) inference for real-time state estimation and autonomous burn planning in <2.0 ms.
      • Node 2 (ESP32 Comms Coprocessor): High-throughput dual UART / WebSocket frame router packaging 24-variable flight telemetry at 50 Hz.

      3. Symplectic Hamiltonian Neural Surrogate (PINN)

      • Enforces conservation of orbital Hamiltonian energy directly into the loss landscape for long-duration stability.
      • Sub-2ms real-time collision avoidance boot-burn calculation for dense mega-constellations.

      4. Cartographic Realism & Operations

      • Esri World Imagery multi-resolution satellite mapping for real city flyovers.
      • Real-time ground station AOS/LOS passes, elevation horizons, and RF Doppler shifts.
